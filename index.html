Your Trusted Legal Partners

Claire & Alubi Advocates is a forward-thinking law firm founded by two accomplished legal practitioners with complementary expertise. Our firm brings together extensive experience in litigation, corporate law, and alternative dispute resolution to serve a diverse clientele ranging from individuals to multinational corporations.

We pride ourselves on our strategic approach to legal challenges, combining rigorous analysis with practical business sense. Whether navigating complex commercial transactions or resolving high-stakes disputes, we remain committed to achieving optimal outcomes for our clients.

Partner & Founder

Claire Kituyi

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

A senior associate holding a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B Hons) from Moi University and a Master of Arts in Diplomacy, Development and International Security from Daystar University. Beverline Sherryl Alubi

Commercial, Corporate & Business Law
