Senior Associate

Claire Kituyi Litigation & Dispute Resolution

A distinguished legal practitioner with extensive experience in Corporate & Commercial Law, Arbitration, and Cross-Border Dispute Resolution across East Africa and beyond.

Schedule Consultation Practice Areas

Dedicated Legal Excellence Across Multiple Jurisdictions

Claire Kituyi is a senior associate holding a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B Hons) Degree from Moi University and a Master of Arts in Diplomacy, Development and International Security from Daystar University. She is a qualified arbitrator under the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators and holds certification in criminal litigation by Lawyers Without Borders.

With extensive experience advising clients on Corporate & Commercial transactional matters, her practice extends to Private Equity, Buy-Outs & Investment Transactions, Project Financing, Telecommunications, Media Technology, e-Commerce, Tax Law, Employment & Labor Relations, and General Corporate Commercial Practice.

As part of the litigation department, she has acted and advised in various areas including Commercial Litigation & Dispute Resolutions, Intellectual Property Law, ICT Law, Real Estate Development & Construction Law, Procurement Law, Arbitration & Alternative Dispute Resolution, Constitutional and Administrative Law, Environmental and Land Law, Health Law, Company Law, Insolvency Law and Employment Law.
